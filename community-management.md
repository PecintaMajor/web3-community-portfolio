# Community Management

## Objective

Build an active, welcoming, and well-organized community where members feel comfortable participating.

## Responsibilities

- Welcome new members
- Answer community questions
- Monitor conversations
- Encourage discussions
- Share announcements
- Support community events
- Collect member feedback
- Escalate issues when necessary

## Daily Workflow

1. Check important channels
2. Welcome new members
3. Respond to questions
4. Start or support conversations
5. Monitor community activity
6. Collect useful feedback
7. Share relevant updates
